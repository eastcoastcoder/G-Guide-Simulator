# Features

## Play IPTV channels in the app without LiveChannels

**Status:** Open  
**Area:** IPTV playback / guide integration

### Desired behavior

The app should use the saved IPTV playlist and EPG source to provide the guide and play channels on its own. It should not require the LiveChannels app or an external native TV app to watch IPTV.

Selecting a channel should start playback within G-Guide and keep the user in the app, rather than switching to native TV or another app. The guide should continue to use the saved EPG data to show programme information for the IPTV channels.
